Bluetooth warning: iOS CoreBluetooth restricts BLE background scanning. When your app goes to background, BLE scanning stops within seconds. Users would have to keep the app in foreground permanently — which defeats the purpose of a control surface. Every competitor who shipped BT-first eventually pivoted. Don't repeat that mistake.
