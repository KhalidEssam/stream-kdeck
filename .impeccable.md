## Design Context

### Users
Power users who live at their computers — developers, gamers, streamers, video editors, writers. They run multiple apps simultaneously, use custom shortcuts extensively, and are comfortable with technical software. Context: at a desk, often late at night or in dark-lit setups. They want a tool that feels as capable as they are.

### Brand Personality
**KDeck** — the name is short, sharp, keyboard-adjacent. Three words: **commanding**, **precise**, **premium**.
The interface should feel like a pro tool you paid good money for — not a consumer product trying to look sleek. Think: Ableton Live's control surface UI, a mechanical keyboard firmware configurator, a professional audio router. No marketing fluff. Every pixel earns its place.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Dark mode only** — the audience is at their computer in low light
- **Color palette**: Deep near-black `#090910` background with purple-indigo accent `#6d5dfc`. Neutrals tinted toward the purple hue. Accent is rare and deliberate.
- **Typography**: Barlow Condensed (display/headings — commanding, space-efficient, authoritative at large sizes) paired with Figtree (body/UI — clean geometric, readable, modern without being generic)
- **Motion**: Purposeful. Scroll-reveal with stagger for content sections. Spring physics for interactive elements. AnimatePresence for menu transitions. Always respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Anti-references**: No teal SaaS gradients, no rounded pastel cards, no glassmorphism decorating every surface, no emoji icons

### Design Principles
1. **Tools over toys** — the interface communicates capability, not friendliness
2. **Restraint as quality signal** — less decoration, more precision; whitespace is confidence
3. **Hierarchy through contrast** — Barlow Condensed at large scale against tight Figtree body creates clear rhythm
4. **Motion serves state** — animations reveal transitions and scroll position, never decorate idly
5. **Dark is the default** — not a "dark mode option", the product lives in this palette
